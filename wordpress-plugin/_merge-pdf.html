    Merge PDF — frequently asked questions

    How do I merge PDF files for free?

    Add your PDFs above, put them in order and click “Merge PDF”. It is free with no limits or watermark.

    Does merging reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied without re-compression, so quality is identical to the originals.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Encrypted PDFs must be unlocked first; the tool will tell you which file is protected.

    How many PDFs can I merge?

    There is no fixed limit; it depends only on your device memory.

    Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

    Yes, it works in mobile browsers on Android and iOS.
