How to convert JPG to PDF — quick answer

To convert JPG to PDF, add your images above, drag them into the right order, choose A4 or “Same as image” page size and click Convert to PDF. All images are combined into one PDF with correct rotation for phone photos. JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and BMP are supported.

JPG to PDF in 3 easy steps

  1. Add images: Select or drop JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF or BMP files — as many as you like.
  2. Arrange order: Drag files or use the ↑ ↓ buttons to set the page order.
  3. Create PDF: Choose page size & margins, convert and download one PDF.

JPG to PDF — frequently asked questions

How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?

Select all images at once (or add them one by one), arrange the order and click Convert. You get a single PDF with one image per page.

Will image quality be reduced?

No. Standard JPG and PNG images are embedded at their original quality.

Can I convert photos from my phone?

Yes — open this page in your mobile browser, tap “Choose files” and select photos from your gallery.

How can I reduce the PDF size?

Use smaller images, or choose A4 page size; very large camera photos produce larger PDFs.
