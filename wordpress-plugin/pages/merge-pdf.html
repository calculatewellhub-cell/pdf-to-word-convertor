How to merge PDF files — quick answer

To merge PDF files, add two or more PDFs above, drag them into the order you want and click Merge PDF. All pages are combined into one PDF without any loss of quality — text, links, forms and images are copied exactly. The merge happens in your browser, so no file is uploaded.

Merge PDF in 3 easy steps

  1. Add PDF files: Select two or more PDFs, or drag them into the box.
  2. Reorder: Drag the files or use ↑ ↓ to arrange the final order.
  3. Merge & download: Click Merge PDF and download your combined document.

Merge PDF: everything you need to know

Merging PDFs is useful for combining scanned pages, assembling a job application (CV + certificates), joining invoices for accounting, or putting chapters of a report together. Unlike many online tools, this one never uploads your files — the pages are copied inside your browser using the open-source pdf-lib engine, keeping text searchable and quality untouched.

Need to turn images into a PDF first? Use JPG to PDF, then merge. Need only some pages? Use Split PDF.

Merge PDF — frequently asked questions

How do I merge PDF files for free?

Add your PDFs above, put them in order and click “Merge PDF”. It is free with no limits or watermark.

Does merging reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied without re-compression, so quality is identical to the originals.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Encrypted PDFs must be unlocked first; the tool will tell you which file is protected.

How many PDFs can I merge?

There is no fixed limit; it depends only on your device memory.

Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

Yes, it works in mobile browsers on Android and iOS.
