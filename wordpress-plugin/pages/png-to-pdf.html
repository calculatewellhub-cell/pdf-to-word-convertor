PNG to PDF — frequently asked questions

Can I merge PNG and JPG into one PDF?

Yes. Add both types together; they will be placed in the order you choose.

Are transparent PNGs supported?

Yes. Transparent areas appear white in the PDF, like on paper.

Is there a limit on the number of images?

No fixed limit — it depends on your device memory.

Does the PDF have a watermark?

Never.
